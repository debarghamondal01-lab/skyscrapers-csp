📖 How to Play

  • Fill the grid so every row and column contains each number exactly once.
  • The numbers on the edges tell you how many buildings you can see from that direction.
  • A taller building hides shorter ones behind it.
  • Click a cell, then click a number to place a building.
  • Use 💡 Hint when stuck.
  • Click ✓ Validate to check your solution.
